Your next phase: results, announcements and student support

Already signed in to your institute CRM? Follow these upgrade steps. Do not run setup.php again.

Back up first. Export your database in phpMyAdmin and copy your existing config.php and storage/ folder to a safe, private location. Do not delete or replace your whole installation folder. Do not upload backups to GitHub.
  1. Pause use of the CRM and its notification worker while updating.
  2. Extract the new ZIP into a separate temporary folder. From that extracted institute-crm folder, copy app/, public/, bin/, docs/, vendor/, composer.json and the help HTML files into your existing project folder, replacing program files when asked. Leave your existing config.php, storage folder, database and installer locks untouched. Avoid creating a second nested institute-crm folder.
  3. Restart Apache (existing staff sessions will require a fresh sign-in), then sign in at your normal staff login as the group owner, then open http://localhost/institute-crm/public/upgrade.php.
  4. Confirm your backup and click Run safe CRM upgrade. It adds academic, fee-schedule, student-service and portal tables, not a new database or owner account. Existing admissions, fees and SMTP settings are preserved.
  5. Open Students. Check that the student has their own correct email. Click Enable student access.
  6. Share your public student-portal link. On your XAMPP computer it is http://localhost/institute-crm/public/student.php. Remote students need your institute's live HTTPS URL; localhost on their phone does not reach your computer.
  7. Test with a student inbox you control. Check their documents and fee balance, then resume the worker.
What students can do: sign in through email OTP; view their own admission/profile, course fee, recorded payments and balance; download their own admission and receipt PDFs, view published results and announcements, and send private support requests. They cannot change their official academic/financial records, make online payments, or see another student's information.

New daily workflows

After upgrading, open Exams & results, Announcements, Student support, Teachers, Batches, Attendance, Fee schedules and Fee reports. Students can see their own finalized attendance and installment schedules. Read docs/OPERATIONS.md for workflow rules and the production checklist. Read docs/STUDENT-SERVICES.md for results, notices and support, and docs/RECOVERY.md for backups. Teacher entries are a directory, not login accounts.

Access and email safety

No student is enabled automatically. Each portal account needs a unique personal email; shared family emails and multiple portal enrollments under one email are not supported yet. Disabling access revokes existing sessions and pending codes. Changing the student's email disables access until staff checks it and enables it again. Enabling access does not automatically send an invitation—share the link securely.

If the upgrade reports an error

Check that Apache and the upgrade page use your existing configuration, and that the database user has CREATE/INDEX permission. The additive migration can be rerun after correcting permissions. Do not delete your database or installer locks. If you must roll back program files, keep a backup of the working application as well as its database.

For a fresh install, use START-HERE.html. For full details, read docs/OPERATIONS.md and docs/STUDENT-PORTAL.md. The package is tested with SQLite and private mail capture; validate MySQL, Gmail delivery and supported browsers on your own server before real student rollout.
